About Claim Checker

Fighting misinformation through neutral AI analysis.

Our Mission

Claim Checker was developed as part of the Grok System by Joseph Herrmann. In an era of rapid information flow, our goal is to provide a neutral, third-party assessment of digital claims. We don't tell you what to think; we show you how a machine interprets the balance and bias of a statement.

How It Works

1. Input

Raw text is sanitized and prepared for the model.

2. Inference

BART Neural Network weighs text against factual benchmarks.

3. Verdict

Scores are translated into a human-readable report.

The Tech Stack

  • Flask (Python): Our core engine, handling asynchronous requests and routing.
  • Hugging Face API: Provides the "brain power" using the BART-Large-MNLI model.
  • Grok Logic: A custom weighting system to calculate the "Bias Intensity" meter.

Note: Our AI uses a massive 1GB neural network. If it's been idle, it may take 20 seconds to "wake up" for your first analysis.
