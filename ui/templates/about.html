About the Project

Claim Checker is an experimental AI safety tool designed to detect subjective language, emotional manipulation, and potential misinformation in real-time.

Our Mission

In the age of rapid information, distinguishing between **verifiable fact** and **persuasive opinion** is critical. This tool provides an objective "second opinion" to help users consume content more critically.

How It Works

  • Syntax Analysis: We scan for patterns often used in sensationalism (e.g., excessive capitalization, specific punctuation).
  • Sentiment Weighting: Subjective terms ("terrible", "amazing") lower the credibility score, while data-driven terms ("study", "percent") raise it.
  • Source Intelligence: The system estimates the origin of the text (e.g., Academic vs. Social Media) based on linguistic markers.
